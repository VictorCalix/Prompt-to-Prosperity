## What is Prompt to Prosperity?
Prompt to Prosperity is a hands-on training program in the use of superintelligence for entrepreneurs. It aims to empower Honduran entrepreneurs with practical superintelligence skills to strengthen their productivity, improve their digital presence, optimize the management of their businesses and create greater opportunities for economic growth.

Keywords: what is, prompt, prosperity, initiative, project, program, superintelligence

## How long is the program?
The program lasts 10 months and includes 3 in-person meetings and 17 virtual sessions. Sessions take place on Saturdays at 6PM EST, except in-person meetings (11AM EST). Each thematic session lasts 2 hours and each hands-on SI workshop lasts 3 hours.

Keywords: duration, long, months, meetings, in-person, virtual, sessions, workshops, schedule, saturday, time

## What does the program include?
For 10 months you will have access to free subscriptions to Claude and Canva Pro, workshops with national and international experts, one-on-one mentoring with professionals and experts, hands-on training in superintelligence applied to business, networking with other entrepreneurs, professionals and partners, complementary resources and materials, social media visibility for participating businesses, and a program backed by the U.S. Embassy in Honduras.

Keywords: include, includes, benefits, support, tools, claude, canva, mentoring, workshops, experts, resources, networking, visibility

## What will entrepreneurs learn?
The curriculum has 5 modules: AI Foundations (introduction to SI and opportunities for entrepreneurs), Prompt Engineering (how to get better results from SI tools), Marketing & Personal Brand (automating digital content, managing customer data and improving positioning), Productivity & Operations (process automation and optimization) and Data & Decision Making (information analysis for better decisions).

Keywords: curriculum, learn, topics, modules, foundations, prompt engineering, marketing, productivity, operations, data, decisions

## Is there a certificate?
Yes. Participants can earn an official certificate backed by PtP and the U.S. Embassy in Honduras. The certificate is subject to meeting the program requirements.

Keywords: certificate, certification, diploma

## What are the requirements to apply?
Be an entrepreneur with a business currently in operation; be highly motivated to learn, innovate and strengthen your business; have a basic level of English (A1 or higher); have access to a computer and a stable internet connection; be available for the 10 months of the program; commit to completing at least 90% of sessions, activities and assignments; and have a genuine interest in applying what you learn to strengthen your business and make a positive impact on your community.

Keywords: requirements, eligibility, apply, english, computer, internet, availability, commitment, who can

## How are participants selected?
We consider the quality and completeness of the application, motivation to be part of the program, level of commitment, the growth potential of the business, interest in implementing SI, clarity on how they will apply what they learn and interest in making a positive impact on their community. Cohort 1 received 212 applications and 40 participants were selected.

Keywords: selection, selected, criteria, how many, applications, seats
